Terms of use

Linked Translator · Updated 25 October 2026

Purpose

Linked Translator supports multilingual conversations in Discord through linked-channel translation, corrections and glossary rules. The Google integration is operated for file exchange and separate training work.

Use and administration

Use the bot only in channels where you have permission, follow Discord's rules and respect other participants. Server administrators are responsible for selecting linked channels, informing their communities and managing learning participation. Read the privacy policy before enabling or contributing to shared learning.

Translation accuracy and availability

Translations, model reviews and learned rules can be wrong or omit context. Review consequential information with an appropriately qualified person. The service is provided as available; uptime, GPU availability and uninterrupted training are not guaranteed.

Third-party services

Discord, Google Drive, Google Colab, Oracle and GitHub have their own terms and limits. Access to free resources depends on those providers. These terms do not authorize a paid subscription or paid resource deployment.

Control and support

Administrators can manage shared learning using the bot's learning commands. The operator can revoke Google account access, pause training and roll back shared rule releases. Contact the operator through Discord in a server where the bot is installed for support or privacy requests.
